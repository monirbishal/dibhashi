About Dibhashi

Dibhashi is an AI‑powered platform that transforms English videos into natural‑sounding Bangla dubs in just a few clicks. By combining state‑of‑the‑art speech and language models, it preserves the speaker’s emotion, timing, and clarity throughout the process.

Seamless Pipeline

  • Extract audio from any YouTube link
  • Transcribe English speech (Whisper ASR)
  • Translate text to Bangla (fine‑tuned NMT)
  • Synthesize Bangla speech with cloned voice (VITS TTS)
  • Merge and sync new audio back into the video (FFmpeg)

Built for Everyone

  • Content Creators looking to reach Bengali audiences
  • Educators & Learners who prefer Bangla instruction
  • Developers & Researchers exploring multilingual speech tools

Dibhashi is open‑source, modular, and ready for you to explore or extend.
